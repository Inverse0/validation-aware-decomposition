HOW TO JUDGE THIS BLOCK. It is one step of a larger query that was cut into steps: the steps that read this relation apply their own conditions, and the steps it reads are verified separately (take the facts stated about them as given). Judge it as you would judge a whole query against its question. The requirement above says what the readers need; what it does not mention is not required of this step, and a filter or join that a careful query for the question would apply is acceptable even if the requirement does not mention it. A reliance on one row per entity or on a non-null value is satisfied when the SQL gives it under the schema's keys or under the evident data model; object to duplicates, NULLs, ties or value formats only when the SQL would evidently produce them for this question's data, as you would for a whole query. Reject only for a clear defect against the requirement: a wrong filter, a wrong join, a wrong grouping, a wrong column, a lost population; do not import requirements of the final answer that belong to another step.
